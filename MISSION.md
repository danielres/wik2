# Mission

This project exists to help spaces coordinate with less noise, less screen time, and more real-life connection.

## Core Problem

Many spaces currently organize through chat tools such as Telegram, Discord, Whatsapp,... 

 Chat is useful for:

- announcements
- live logistics
- short-lived discussion

But chat is horrible at:

- durable knowledge
- discoverability
- structure
- reducing repetition

Over time, important knowledge gets buried in years of messages. People spend too much time searching, re-explaining, scrolling, and reacting to notifications.

## Core Goal

Move persistent space knowledge out of chat and into a structured shared memory.

The platform should help people:

- find what they need quickly
- contribute improvements easily
- avoid repeated questions
- reduce notification pressure
- leave the screen sooner
- spend more time coordinating and meeting in real life

## Product Direction

This is not a generic social platform and not a retention-driven product.

It is intentionally built against attention-extractive software. The goal is not to keep users engaged for as long as possible, but to resolve their need as quickly as possible.

In that sense, the project fights harmful tech patterns with better tech:

- structure instead of feed noise
- shared memory instead of repeated chat explanations
- retrieval instead of scrolling
- calm coordination instead of constant engagement

## Main Unit

The main unit is the space.

Examples:

- local communities
- neighborhoods
- student spaces
- hobby spaces
- support spaces
- small companies

Each space needs its own shared space for knowledge, coordination, and light governance.

## What The Wiki Is For

The wiki is the durable knowledge layer for the space.

It should hold:

- practical information
- guides
- recurring resources
- space memory
- reusable content

Chat should remain mostly for:

- announcements
- in-the-moment coordination
- logistics

## V2 Direction

Version 2 is not a change of mission. It is a stronger architecture for the same mission.

The direction of v2 is to make first-class:

- space multitenancy
- strong permission boundaries
- reusable modular content blocks
- separation of content ownership and publication
- collaborative editing safeguards
- maintenance surfaces for space stewards

## Design Filter

A feature is aligned if it helps spaces:

- reduce chat noise
- reduce repeated explanations
- find useful information faster
- collaborate with less friction
- spend less time online
- coordinate more effectively in real life

A feature is probably misaligned if it mainly increases:

- engagement for its own sake
- notification volume
- scrolling
- ambiguity
- performative activity instead of useful coordination
